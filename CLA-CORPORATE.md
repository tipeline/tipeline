# Tipeline Corporate Contributor License Agreement

This agreement lets a company or other organization (the "Corporation") authorize its
employees to contribute to Tipeline. It documents the rights the Corporation grants for
those contributions, so the project can be used, distributed and offered under any terms,
now and in the future. The Corporation keeps the copyright in its contributions; this is a
license, not an assignment. It is adapted from the Apache Software Foundation Corporate CLA.

Each employee who contributes must also sign the [Individual CLA](CLA.md), normally through
the CLA bot on their first pull request.

**To sign:** fill in the details at the end, have an authorized signatory sign it, and send
it to the Maintainer through [github.com/tipeline](https://github.com/tipeline). Keep a copy
for your records.

the Maintainer (the **"Work"**). "Submit" means any form of electronic, verbal or written
communication sent to the Maintainer or its representatives, including pull requests,
issues, mailing lists and discussions, except communication conspicuously marked as "Not a
Contribution".

updated by You from time to time.
