# Tipeline Trademark Policy

The Tipeline source code is open source under the [Apache License 2.0](LICENSE). The
**Tipeline name and logo are not.** They are trademarks of José Daniel Estrada Guzmán, and the
Apache License explicitly does not grant rights to use them (section 6).

This policy explains what you can do with the name without asking, and what needs
permission. Its goal is simple: anyone who sees "Tipeline" should be able to trust that it
is the real project, or something the project has approved.

## You may, without asking

- **Refer to Tipeline truthfully.** Write about it, teach it, review it, compare it, and say
  your product or plugin "works with Tipeline", "for Tipeline" or "is built on Tipeline".
- **Redistribute unmodified official releases** under the Tipeline name.
- **Run Tipeline inside your own organization**, including modified versions, and call it
  Tipeline internally.
- **Name community things** such as meetups, user groups, blog posts, tutorials or plugins
  using "Tipeline" in a descriptive way (for example "Tipeline Madrid Meetup" or
  "Slack plugin for Tipeline"), as long as it is clear they are not official.

## You need written permission to

- **Name a product, fork, distribution or service** "Tipeline" or anything confusingly
  similar (for example "Tipeline Pro", "TipelineCloud", "OpenTipeline").
- **Offer Tipeline as a hosted or managed service** under the Tipeline name.
- **Use the Tipeline logo** in a way that suggests the project endorses, sponsors or is
  affiliated with you.
- **Register domains, company names, app store listings or social accounts** that contain
  "Tipeline".
- **Sell merchandise** with the Tipeline name or logo.

## Forks

Forking is welcome; that is what the license is for. If you distribute a modified version
to others, give it a different name and logo. You may say it is "based on Tipeline".

## Asking for permission

Open an issue or contact the maintainers at
[github.com/tipeline](https://github.com/tipeline). Most reasonable requests are approved.

This policy may be updated as the project grows.
